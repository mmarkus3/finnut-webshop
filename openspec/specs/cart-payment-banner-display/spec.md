## Requirements
### Requirement: Cart page SHALL display Visma Pay banner at bottom
The system SHALL render the image `https://static.vismapay.com/pay_banners/row.png` at the bottom area of cart page.

#### Scenario: Banner is visible below cart content
- **WHEN** cart page is rendered
- **THEN** payment banner image is displayed after cart summary content near bottom of page
