## Why

Information page currently shows only short placeholder content, but customers need full delivery terms visible before ordering decisions. Adding complete terms improves legal clarity and customer trust.

## What Changes

- Replace information page placeholder body with full delivery terms content provided by business.
- Ensure terms are clearly readable on information page with sectioned formatting.
- Keep information page accessible and scrollable for long content.
- Add/update tests to verify terms heading and representative content are rendered.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `information-page-global-footer-link`: Extend information page content requirement to render full delivery terms text.

## Impact

- Affected UI: information page content rendering.
- Affected i18n/content: information page translation/content keys.
- Affected tests: information page rendering assertions.
